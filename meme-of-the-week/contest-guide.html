🔥 How to Enter Meme of the Week

Create your meme, download the finished PNG, then submit it to the active weekly contest. Your meme can be about anything; it does not have to be about SPARKD or the SPARKD community.

  1. Open Meme Forge. Upload an image, add your text or emojis, and finish your meme. Your subject or theme can be anything you want.
  2. Download the finished meme. Use the download/export button to save the PNG you want to enter.
  3. Return to Meme of the Week. Connect the Phantom wallet you want to use for your contest entry.
  4. Click “ENTER MEME OF THE WEEK.” Select your downloaded SPARKD Forge PNG and enter a title.
  5. Submit and approve the entry burn. Your wallet must hold at least 2,000 SPARKD. Phantom will show the required 2,000 SPARKD contest-entry burn for approval.
  6. Wait for confirmation. Keep the page open while the entry is verified. Once successful, your meme is entered into the current weekly contest.

🎲 Equal-Chance Drawing Rules

How the drawing works

  • At the submission deadline, every eligible meme receives exactly one equal chance.
  • The server freezes the eligible submission list and cryptographically shuffles it.
  • Up to three unique finalists are selected, then shuffled again to assign 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place.
  • A single submission cannot occupy more than one prize position.

🏆 Weekly prizes

  • 🥇 1st Place — $15 USD worth of SOL
  • 🥈 2nd Place — $10 USD worth of SOL
  • 🥉 3rd Place — $5 USD worth of SOL
  • The SOL amount is calculated from the USD prize value when payout is prepared.

Contest safeguards

  • Only eligible, verified submissions are included in the drawing.
  • With fewer than three eligible memes, only the available prize positions are awarded.
  • With zero eligible memes, no winner or meme prize payout is created for that contest.
  • Winner selection and weekly contest rollover are handled automatically by the server.
⚠️ Only approve the transaction if Phantom shows exactly what you expect. The contest submission engine verifies the entry burn before finalizing the submission.
