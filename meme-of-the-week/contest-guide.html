🔥 How to Enter Meme of the Week

Create your meme in SPARKD Meme Forge, download the official Forge PNG, then submit it to the active weekly contest.

  1. Open Meme Forge. Upload an image, add your text or emojis, and finish your meme.
  2. Download the finished meme. Use the Forge download/export button so you keep the official SPARKD Forge PNG data required by the contest.
  3. Return to Meme of the Week. Connect the Phantom wallet you want to use for your contest entry.
  4. Click “ENTER MEME OF THE WEEK.” Select your downloaded SPARKD Forge PNG and enter a title.
  5. Submit and approve the entry burn. Your wallet must hold at least 2,000 SPARKD. Phantom will show the required 2,000 SPARKD contest-entry burn for approval.
  6. Wait for confirmation. Keep the page open while the entry is verified. Once successful, your meme is entered into the current weekly contest.

🗳️ Voting Rules

How voting works

  • Voting opens only during the official voting phase and lasts 12 hours after submissions close.
  • No wallet is required to vote beginning with the next weekly contest. When voting opens, the voting window will appear automatically on the contest page.
  • Voting is free: no SPARKD burn, SOL payment, wallet connection, or minimum SPARKD holding is required.
  • Each browser is allowed one vote per weekly contest. Once that vote is recorded, the same browser cannot cast another vote that week.
  • Entrants may vote for another eligible meme, but self-voting is prohibited.
  • Votes must be for an eligible submission in the current contest. Invalid, duplicate, self-vote, or out-of-window requests are rejected by the server.

🎁 Weekly voter reward

  • The $5 USD worth of SOL voter reward is optional and is separate from the vote itself.
  • After a public vote is recorded, a voter may optionally connect Phantom and sign a reward-entry message to enter the drawing.
  • A public vote still counts even if the voter does not connect a wallet.
  • Voters who do not connect and verify a wallet are not entered in the $5 SOL random voter-reward drawing.
  • Each verified reward wallet may receive no more than one drawing entry per weekly contest. Self-votes are not eligible. Reward is void where prohibited.

🏆 How the winner is chosen

  • When voting closes, the eligible meme with the highest verified vote total wins.
  • If eligible memes are tied, the earlier submitted meme wins the tie.
  • At least one valid vote is required to produce a champion. If there are zero valid votes, no artificial winner is created.
  • Winner selection and weekly contest rollover are handled automatically by the server.
⚠️ Only approve the transaction if Phantom shows exactly what you expect. The contest submission engine verifies the Forge meme and entry burn before finalizing the submission.
