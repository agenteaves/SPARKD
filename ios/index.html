SPARKD for iPhone

Create a meme, export it to your iPhone, and enter Meme of the Week using the SPARKD site.

🔥 Open Meme Forge 🏆 Enter Meme of the Week

Add SPARKD to your Home Screen

  1. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap Share, then tap Add to Home Screen. If needed, scroll down in the Share menu.
  3. Tap Add, then launch SPARKD from your Home Screen.

To enter: export your PNG in Meme Forge, open Meme of the Week, choose your PNG, and follow the wallet and burn prompts. A Solana wallet app may be needed to approve a contest entry. Keep your exported PNG until your entry is confirmed.
