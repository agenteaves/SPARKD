Owner approvals

Approve an NFT to make it available in the vault, or withdraw its approval.

Each approval gives a separate delegate permission to transfer that NFT. The checkout server enforces the SPARKD price. A compromised delegate can transfer its approved NFT without payment; withdraw approval here when you want to remove a listing.

These are real Solana mainnet approvals. Your wallet pays SOL network and NFT storage fees. You never need to share your wallet’s private key.
