About the NFT Vault

How do purchases work?

Choose an available NFT and connect Phantom. Review one Solana mainnet transaction that burns the displayed SPARKD price and transfers the NFT to your wallet. Both actions succeed together, or both are rolled back. SOL network fees are separate. On mobile, open this page in Phantom’s browser.

Can I trade my NFT?

Once purchased, you can transfer it or resell it using a service that supports Metaplex Core. Resales outside our purchase system do not automatically burn SPARKD.

What does one of one mean?

We issue one NFT per selected artwork, with no additional editions of that artwork. Solana network fees are separate and paid in SOL.
