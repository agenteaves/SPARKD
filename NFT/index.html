A few things to know

What does one of one mean?

Our issuance policy is one NFT per selected artwork, with no additional editions of that artwork.

Will my NFT be tradable?

Once purchased, you can transfer your NFT or resell it through a service that supports Metaplex Core. Resales outside this purchase system do not automatically burn SPARKD.

What happens to the purchase tokens?

The planned checkout burns the SPARKD instead of sending it to a seller. Solana network fees are separate and paid in SOL.
