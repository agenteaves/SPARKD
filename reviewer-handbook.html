Reviewer Handbook v0.1

Guidelines for fair, consistent, and evidence-based reviews.

Mission

Reviewers protect the integrity of the Proof of Deeds protocol by evaluating evidence fairly, consistently, and without bias.

Reviewer Principles

  • Remain objective.
  • Review evidence, not personalities.
  • Protect participant privacy.
  • Treat every submission equally.
  • Request additional evidence when necessary.
  • Document concerns clearly.

Review Checklist

  • Is the claim clearly described?
  • Does the evidence support the claim?
  • Does the evidence appear authentic?
  • Is the evidence relevant?
  • Does the submission follow the Safety Rules?

Possible Decisions

Verified

The available evidence reasonably supports the claimed deed.

Needs More Evidence

The claim may be valid, but additional supporting information is needed.

Rejected

The evidence is insufficient or the submission violates protocol requirements.

Conflict of Interest

Reviewers should avoid evaluating submissions where they have a personal relationship or another conflict that could affect impartiality.

Privacy

Only information necessary for verification should be reviewed or retained. Personal information should be protected whenever possible.
