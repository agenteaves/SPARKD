Swap Fee Console

A local prototype for an app-level SPARKD fee attached to a cryptocurrency swap routed through a SPARKD-integrated app. The app fee can be paid in SPARKD while the normal Solana network fee stays in SOL.

Demo mode: no Kora paymaster is needed for this fee model. This console does not connect a wallet, create a swap, transfer SPARKD, or submit a transaction.
