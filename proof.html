What is Proof of Deeds?

Proof of Deeds is a transparent system designed to record verified acts of service, kindness, and community impact. The purpose is not recognition of individuals, but preserving a trustworthy record of actions that create positive change.

How Verification Works

1. Submit

A deed is submitted with supporting information and evidence.

2. Review

Community validators review whether the evidence supports the claim.

3. Verify

Approved deeds become permanent records of verified impact.
